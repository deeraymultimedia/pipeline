EVERYDAY ESSENTIALS

Everything in its place. Everyone in the loop.

From the morning notice to a quiet word with the concierge, the everyday running of your building sits in one calm, private place.

  1. 01

    Notices & events

    Building news and upcoming events from your property team. Neighbours see how many people are going, not who.

  2. 02

    Shared facilities

    Book the gym, lounge or guest suite in a few taps. Each space shows its capacity, booking length and house guidance before you confirm.

  3. 03

    Private building care

    Report a problem in your home or a shared area, with photos if they help, then follow every private update.

  4. 04

    A direct line to your team

    Message your property team and concierge directly. Every conversation stays between you and the people who look after your building.

  5. 05

    Building marketplace

    Offer, find and lend things between verified residents. You appear as “A neighbour”, never by name or apartment.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your name and apartment stay between you and your property team. Neighbours can’t look you up.

Listed as “A neighbour”

Marketplace listings never show your name, apartment or contact details. Enquiries stay in your private inbox.

A 24-hour backstop

Reports go to your building’s property team first. If nothing has happened within 24 hours, 2Gaff steps in.

No payments taken

Exchanges are arranged between verified residents. 2Gaff never handles money between neighbours.
